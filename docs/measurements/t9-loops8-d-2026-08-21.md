# T9 — 8 loops at 1024×3, condition D (2026-08-21)

`~/t9-loops8-d.log` on raspberrypi2. n=15, 60 s per run, condition D (full stack),
8 loops recorded and playing. `meter_live=1` on every run.

## Result

**0.00 xruns/60 s. 15/15 clean.** DSP median ~39%, p99 ~41–42%.

**The "up to 8 loops" claim survives at condition D.** It was previously measured only at
condition B and was the weakest link in the shipping story. It is now measured on the
configuration that actually runs.

## This refutes the fixed-cost reading of the soak

The soak doc read B→D as a **fixed +0.80/min independent of period**, on the strength of
+0.86 at 512 and +0.80 at 1024. That was wrong, and this run is what breaks it. Collecting
every 1024 cell:

| loops | condition B | condition D | B→D |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.00 | — | — |
| 4 | 0.00 | — | — |
| 8 | 0.00 | **0.00** | **0.00** |
| 16 | 0.13 | 0.93 | +0.80 |

**The stack costs nothing at 8 loops and +0.80/min at 16.** It is not a fixed per-minute
cost. It is not a pure loop-count cost either — condition B barely moves across the same
range (0.00 → 0.13). It is an **interaction**: the stack is free until the loop load is
high, and then it is expensive.

## And the 512 structural cost is period-specific

At 512, loop count is irrelevant (T4a, condition B): 0 loops 3.00, 4 loops 1.33, 8 loops
2.67, 16 loops 3.40 — non-monotonic, flat inside noise. Merely having sooperlooper in the
graph costs ~3/min there **regardless of how much work it is doing**.

At 1024 the same client with 8 loops costs **zero**.

So there are two independent terms, and conflating them produced the fixed-cost error:

- **Structural** — the extra process hop in JACK's serial chain. Dominant at 512, where
  the deadline is tight enough that an extra scheduling handoff is fatal whatever the
  client is computing. Invisible at 1024.
- **Load** — actual loop DSP work. Invisible at 8 loops. Appears at 16, and only when the
  rest of the stack is also present.

The earlier claim that "sooperlooper is 71% of the cost" is a **512-specific** observation,
not a general one. It should not be carried to 1024 conclusions.

## What this changes

- **1024×3, condition D, 8 loops is clean at n=15 and is the shipping configuration.** The
  headline claim no longer needs the condition-B footnote.
- **16 loops is where it breaks**, and the break needs both high loop load and the full
  stack. That is the cell to investigate, not 8.
- **T7a still worth running.** But its 256 cells will be dominated by the structural term,
  which is what T7a's cushion hypothesis is actually about — so the reading is cleaner
  than expected, not muddier.
- **T10 (wakeup delay) is now better targeted**: the structural term is the thing to
  instrument, and 512 condition B is where it is largest and easiest to catch.
