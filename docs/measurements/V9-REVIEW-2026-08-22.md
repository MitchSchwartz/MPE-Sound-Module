## Retracted: my "ceilings are systematically optimistic" hypothesis

`V8-REVIEW-ceiling-is-optimistic.md` predicted the ramp-derived counts would fall under longer
confirms. **They did not.** Crystals @ 3 and Cloud Horn @ 5 both hold **0 xruns over 60 s** at
their ramp-derived counts.

**The falsifier was stated in advance and it fired. Record it as such.**

## The actual defect: the ramp is noisy at the knee, not biased

V8-a reported Cloud Horn clean at **7**. V9's ramp — **same 8 s window, same patch** — saw
**xruns at 7** and settled on 5.

**Same probe, same count, different answer.** That is variance, not bias.

**Consequence for the fix:** lengthening the confirm does not address it. **Repeat the probe
at the candidate count (n >= 3) and take the worst**, or step back one from the first clean
result. A single 8 s probe near the knee is close to a coin flip, because the event rate there
is low by definition.

## Closed Hat — ramp under-counts; not duration sensitivity

| test | window | xruns |
|---|---|---:|
| V9-a ramp `_xruns_delta` @ 15 voices | 8 s | **0** |
| V9-c confirm harness @ 15 voices | **8 s** | **275** |
| V9-a confirm @ 15 voices | 60 s | 2101 |
| V9-c ceiling search (confirm harness) | 60 s | **max clean = 5** |

Same load generator (`midi-load-hold.py`) and **same 8 s window** — ramp reads **0**, confirm
harness reads **275**. Probe length is not the explanation.

**Root cause (working):** `measure-capacity-ramp.sh` `_xruns_delta` is a bare meter delta
without the strict restart / window discipline in `measure-latency-run.sh`. High-load cells
can read clean in the ramp and fail immediately in confirm.

**Consequence:** V8-a ramp numbers are **screening only**. Policy ceilings require
`measure-confirm-at-voices.sh` (or ramp fixed to share confirm counting — V10-b).

Percussive patches (Closed Hat, Kick) may still need a separate metric; do not trust ramp
rows for them even after the counting fix.
