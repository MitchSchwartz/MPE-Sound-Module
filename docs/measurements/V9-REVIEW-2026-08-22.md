## Ship it: `1024 x 2` is free at a verified-clean load

| config | xruns (3 x 45 s) | DSP median |
|---|---|---|
| `1024 x 2` | **0 / 0 / 0** | ~57% |
| `1024 x 3` | **0 / 0 / 0** | ~57% |

Cloud Horn @ 5 voices, condition A, strict mode, governor off.

**This is the first comparison of `x2` vs `x3` below the overrun knee.** V3 (100% DSP) and
V8-b (~80% DSP, both arms overrunning) were overload arms and could not answer the question —
at overload the deadline binds identically regardless of cushion, which is exactly what they
measured.

**Result: 64.0 ms -> 42.7 ms, no measured cost, no change to Surge's 21.3 ms deadline.**

**Before making it the default**, confirm at **2-3 more patches** across the weight range at
their verified-clean counts. One patch at one voice count is a rate claim with n=1 patch.
The cushion halves from 42.7 ms to 21.3 ms, and while nothing we have measured comes close to
consuming that, the confirmation is ~15 minutes.

## Retracted: my "ceilings are systematically optimistic" hypothesis

`V8-REVIEW-ceiling-is-optimistic.md` predicted the ramp-derived counts would fall under longer
confirms. **They did not.** Crystals @ 3 and Cloud Horn @ 5 both hold **0 xruns over 60 s** at
their ramp-derived counts.

**The falsifier was stated in advance and it fired. Record it as such.**

## The actual defect: the ramp is noisy at the knee, not biased

V8-a reported Cloud Horn clean at **7**. V9's ramp — **same 8 s window, same patch** — saw
**xruns at 7** and settled on 5.

**Same probe, same count, different answer.** That is variance, not bias.

**Consequence for the fix:** lengthening the confirm does not address it. **Repeat the probe
at the candidate count (n >= 3) and take the worst**, or step back one from the first clean
result. A single 8 s probe near the knee is close to a coin flip, because the event rate there
is low by definition.

## Unexplained — do not file this as duration sensitivity

| Closed Hat | ramp @ 15 | 60 s confirm @ 15 |
|---|---|---|
| result | **clean** | **2101 xruns** |

2101 xruns / 60 s = **~35/s**. An 8 s probe at that rate should have recorded **~280**. It
recorded **zero** — a discrepancy of roughly **350x**. Probe length cannot produce that;
something differs between the ramp and the confirm beyond the window.

**Two candidates, both cheap:**

1. **The load generators differ.** The confirm uses `midi-load-hold.py` — *hold*. If the ramp
   does not sustain notes, its nominal voice count never becomes actual simultaneous
   polyphony, and the two arms are not the same load at all.
2. **The metric is ill-defined for percussive patches.** Closed Hat has a very short envelope;
   "15 sustained voices" is not a state it can occupy, and holding a key changes nothing.
   **Kick** appears in V8-a's bounded-worst list for the same reason. Percussive patches
   filed alongside pads produce numbers that look comparable and are not.

**Until this is resolved, Closed Hat is not evidence about anything**, and the **38 censored
rows** in V8-a stay unusable — now with a demonstration that they can be wrong by orders of
magnitude, not by a voice or two.
